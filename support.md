## iCloud Sync
{: #icloud-sync}

If your countdowns are not syncing across your devices, follow these steps on each device.

### 1. Verify your iCloud settings

#### iPhone or iPad

- Open the **Settings** app.
- Tap your name near the top.
- Make sure the Apple Account shown is the same one used on your other devices.
- Tap **iCloud**.
- Find **Countdowns** in **Saved to iCloud** (formerly **Apps Using iCloud**) and make sure it is enabled.
- Make sure **iCloud Drive** is enabled and that Countdowns is enabled under **Apps Syncing to iCloud Drive**.

#### Mac

- Open the Apple menu () and choose **System Settings**.
- Click your name near the top.
- Make sure the Apple Account shown is the same one used on your other devices.
- Click **iCloud**.
- Click **See All** under **Saved to iCloud** (formerly **Show More Apps** under **Apps Using iCloud**).
- Find **Countdowns** in the list and make sure it is enabled.
- Make sure **iCloud Drive** is enabled and that Countdowns is enabled under **Apps Syncing to iCloud Drive**.

### 2. Check your iCloud storage

Syncing requires enough available iCloud storage. Check that your account is not out of storage.

### 3. Update your software

iCloud sync works best with current software. Install the latest version of Countdowns on every device, along with the latest available versions of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

### 4. Restart your devices

Restart each affected device, then open Countdowns again and allow a little time for changes to sync.

## Apple Watch Sync
{: #apple-watch-sync}

If your countdowns are not syncing to Apple Watch, try these steps.

### 1. Restart your devices

Restart both your iPhone and Apple Watch. This typically resolves temporary sync issues.

### 2. Update your software

Install the latest version of Countdowns and update both iOS and watchOS to the latest versions available for your devices.

## Widgets
{: #widgets}

### Installation guides

- [How to add and edit widgets on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118610)
- [Change your iPhone wallpaper](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102638)
- [Add and customize widgets on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl52be5da5/mac)

If Countdowns does not appear in the widget gallery, restart your device and make sure you have opened the app at least once.

If a widget is not updating, try the following:

### 1. Restart your device

Restarting your device typically resolves temporary widget issues.

### 2. Update your software

Install the latest version of Countdowns and update iOS, iPadOS, or macOS to the latest version available for your device.

## Family Sharing
{: #family-sharing}

Countdowns supports Family Sharing for subscriptions and in-app purchases. Make sure purchase sharing is configured for your Apple family group and that each family member is signed in with the correct Apple Account.

## Supported OS Versions
{: #supported-os-versions}

Countdowns supports at least the current major operating-system version and the two preceding major versions on each supported Apple platform.

## Refunds
{: #refunds}

If something is wrong, please give us a chance to make it right. Only Apple can issue refunds for App Store purchases. To request one, follow [Apple's refund process](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).
