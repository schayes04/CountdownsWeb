## Count down to more than Christmas morning

See [how many days are left until Christmas]({{ '/en-ca/when-is/christmas/' | relative_url }}) with our free web countdown, including Christmas Eve, the first Sunday of Advent, and Christmas dates for upcoming years.

The Christmas season includes travel, shopping, decorating, meals, school breaks, shipping deadlines, and family plans. Countdowns lets each important date have its own timer instead of hiding everything inside one calendar view.

Use a main Christmas countdown for the day itself, then create smaller countdowns for the dates that help the season run smoothly.

## Put a Christmas countdown widget on your iPhone

1. **Add Christmas in Countdowns.** Choose December 25 and set it to repeat every year, so it's ready again next season.
2. **Add it to your Lock Screen.** Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the widget area and choose Countdowns.
3. **Add it to your Home Screen.** Touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Countdowns.

The [Lock Screen countdown widget guide]({{ '/en-ca/lock-screen-countdown-widget/' | relative_url }}) has more detail, and the [Mac countdown widget guide]({{ '/en-ca/mac-countdown-widget/' | relative_url }}) shows how to keep Christmas on your desktop.

## Christmas countdown ideas

- **Christmas Eve**, for families who open presents or have the big dinner on the 24th.
- **The first Sunday of Advent**, to start an Advent calendar or light the first candle.
- **The last day to post or ship gifts**, so nothing arrives late.
- **The last day of school or work** before the break.
- **The trip home**, with its own reminder the day before you leave.
- **Christmas dinner**, with a shopping countdown a few days earlier.
- **New Year's Eve**, so the next countdown is ready when Christmas is over.

## Keep the countdown where people can see it

A Christmas countdown is fun when it is visible. Add a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, choose seasonal colours, and update the units as the date gets closer.
