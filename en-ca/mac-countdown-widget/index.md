## A countdown app that feels at home on the Mac

Countdowns isn't a web page or a stretched phone app on your Mac. It opens in its own window, lists every countdown with the days, hours, and minutes left, and keeps counting while you work.

- Press **Command-N** to add a countdown, and **Command-Comma** for settings.
- Search the toolbar to find a countdown in a long list.
- Group countdowns into lists, like Work, Trips, or Holidays.
- Open finished countdowns with **Shift-Command-A** from the View menu.
- Choose units, colours, icons, and repeat options for each countdown.

## Desktop widgets you design yourself

A countdown helps most when you can see it. On a Mac, the desktop and Notification Centre are where a quick glance fits best: between meetings, while you're writing, or when you're planning the next trip.

Choose **Window > Widgets** to design a custom widget. Pick a layout, then set its colours and units, and add a photo of the place or the person you're counting down to. The Custom widget in the macOS widget gallery shows the widgets you've saved. The Classic widget shows a single countdown or a whole list with no setup.

## The same countdowns on every device

Countdowns works on Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Create a countdown on any of them and it appears on the others through iCloud. Keep work deadlines on your Mac desktop and personal dates on your iPhone Home Screen, or show the same countdown everywhere.

## Good countdowns for a Mac desktop

- Product launches, releases, and project deadlines
- Exams, semester ends, and graduation
- Vacations, flights, and the last day before time off
- Birthdays, anniversaries, and weddings
- Christmas, New Year, and other celebrations

If a widget doesn't appear in the gallery, open Countdowns once and restart your Mac. For more help, see [Add and customise widgets on Mac]({% include apple-support-url.html key="mac_widgets" %}).
