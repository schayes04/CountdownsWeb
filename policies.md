## Privacy Policy
{: #privacy-policy}

**Last updated August 17, 2026**

We value your information and privacy. Countdowns does not track or collect personally identifiable information from its users, and we do our best to limit the number of third parties we rely on.

- Payments for Countdowns in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed through the App Store, so we never have access to your credit card information. Countdowns includes the RevenueCat SDK. Please refer to [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
- The ad-supported version of Countdowns includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK with AdMob enabled. Please refer to [Google's AdMob privacy information](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en).
- Data synchronization is provided through iCloud using Apple's CloudKit. CloudKit is fully encrypted, and all synced data lives on Apple's infrastructure. No one besides you has access to your data.
- By using Countdowns and making in-app purchases, you consent to our sharing data about your usage and consumption of purchased content with Apple when needed to help resolve a refund request. This information may include details about how you accessed and interacted with the purchased content. It is shared so Apple can make an informed decision regarding the refund request.

We ensure that refund-related data sharing complies with Apple's policies and occurs only when necessary to process your request.

## Terms of Use
{: #terms-of-use}

**Last updated August 17, 2026**

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can cancel at any time through the platform where you made the purchase. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited if you purchase a subscription.
