## A countdown you can see without opening an app

A countdown helps most when you can see it. On a Mac, the desktop and Notification Center are where a quick glance fits best: between meetings, while you're writing, or when you're planning the next trip.

With a Countdowns widget, the days left until a launch, a deadline, a vacation, or a birthday stay visible without opening a calendar or switching apps.

## The same countdowns on every device

Countdowns works on Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Create a countdown on any of them and it appears on the others through iCloud. Keep work deadlines on your Mac desktop and personal dates on your iPhone Home Screen, or show the same countdown everywhere.

## Good countdowns for a Mac desktop

- Product launches, releases, and project deadlines
- Exams, semester ends, and graduation
- Vacations, flights, and the last day before time off
- Birthdays, anniversaries, and weddings
- Christmas, New Year, and other celebrations

If a widget doesn't appear in the gallery, open Countdowns once and restart your Mac. For more help, see [Add and customize widgets on Mac]({% include apple-support-url.html key="mac_widgets" %}).
